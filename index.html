Intelligence for Everyone, Everywhere.

At Sophr, we are exploring new forms of AI that can expand what machines can understand, create, and accomplish. We envision a future where these advances become more general, adaptable, and private, and can be tailored to a wide range of needs and contexts. Our research seeks new ways to turn breakthroughs into practical tools that can work across devices, environments, and applications. In the long term, we aim to advance toward artificial general intelligence and make its benefits accessible to everyone, everywhere.
