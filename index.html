Intelligence for Everyone.

At Sophr, we are exploring new forms of intelligence that can expand what machines can understand, create, and accomplish. We believe the future will be defined by intelligence that is more capable, general, and adaptable, with the ability to understand and interact across modalities while remaining private and customizable to each person’s needs and data. Our research explores new ways of learning, reasoning, and interacting with intelligence to build systems that can serve a wide range of people and applications. Our long term goal is to advance the frontier toward artificial general intelligence and make increasingly powerful intelligence accessible to everyone.
